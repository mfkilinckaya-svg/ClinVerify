About ClinVerify

ClinVerify is an open-source, single-file clinical laboratory verification tool developed at Helse Møre og Romsdal HF (HMR), Department of Medical Biochemistry, Norway. It implements CLSI and EFLM protocols for method verification, precision assessment, bias evaluation, method comparison, and reference interval verification.

Developed for open-source publication on GitHub. Version 2906.
